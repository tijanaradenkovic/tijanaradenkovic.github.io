Research

Higher gauge theory, quantum gravity & topological field theory

My research focuses on higher gauge theories as a framework for quantum gravity, with particular emphasis on 2-groups, 3-groups, 2BF and 3BF theories, gauge symmetry, state-sum models and topological quantum field theory.

Overview

Research focus

My work explores higher-categorical generalizations of gauge theory and their applications to gravity, matter and quantum spacetime. In this framework, ordinary gauge groups are replaced by higher structures such as 2-groups and 3-groups, leading naturally to 2BF and 3BF topological field theories.

A central goal is to formulate gravity and matter in a BF-type language adapted to non-perturbative quantization. My research spans the classical formulation of constrained higher BF theories, their Hamiltonian and gauge structure, and their discrete state-sum and topological quantum field theory formulations.

Main themes

Research directions

Classical formulation

Gravity and matter as constrained 2BF and 3BF theories

Higher BF theories provide a common framework for describing gravity and matter. My work includes constrained 2BF and 3BF formulations of Yang–Mills, Klein–Gordon, Dirac, Weyl and Majorana fields interacting with Einstein–Cartan gravity. The resulting formulation separates a topological sector from the constraints that introduce local physical degrees of freedom, making it suitable for covariant spin-foam quantization.

Canonical structure

Hamiltonian structure and gauge symmetry of 3BF theory

A major part of my research concerns the canonical structure of 3BF theory. I have studied the Hamiltonian formulation of the theory for a generic semistrict Lie 3-group using the Dirac procedure, including its constraints and complete gauge symmetry.

This analysis also clarifies the relation between higher gauge transformations, local translations and spacetime diffeomorphisms.

State sums & TQFT

3BF state sums, topological invariants and TQFT

I study the path-integral quantization of topological 3BF theory and its discrete formulation on triangulated four-manifolds. The corresponding 3-group state sum is invariant under Pachner moves and defines a topological invariant of four-dimensional manifolds.

Extending this construction to manifolds with boundary leads naturally to a topological quantum field theory: the 3BF state sum defines a functor from the category of cobordisms to the category of Hilbert spaces and can be shown explicitly to satisfy Atiyah’s axioms for a TQFT.

Gauge structure

Gauge symmetry and diffeomorphisms in higher BF theories

I have also studied Henneaux–Teitelboim gauge transformations and their role in the full gauge structure of higher BF theories. Although these transformations are trivial on shell, they are important for understanding the structure of the gauge group and, in particular, how spacetime diffeomorphisms arise within generally covariant theories.

Representation theory

Representation theory of 2-groups and 3-groups

I am also interested in the representation theory of higher groups, in particular 2-groups and 3-groups, and in the role that higher-group representations may play in higher gauge theories, state-sum models and quantum gravity.
