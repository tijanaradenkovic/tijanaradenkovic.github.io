Research

Quantum geometry beyond ordinary gauge theory

My work explores higher gauge structures, topological field theories and discrete formulations of quantum gravity, with a particular focus on 2-groups, 3-groups and 3BF theory.

Overview

Research focus

I study extensions of ordinary gauge theory in which parallel transport is described not only along paths, but also over higher-dimensional objects. These structures naturally lead to higher groups, higher connections and BF-type topological field theories.

A central theme of my work is the 3BF theory associated with a Lie 3-group: its gauge symmetry and Hamiltonian structure, its coupling to matter, its discrete state-sum formulation, and its interpretation as a topological quantum field theory.

Main themes

Research directions

Higher geometry

Higher gauge theory & higher groups

2-groups and 3-groups provide the algebraic framework for generalized gauge theories with connections of several form degrees. I am interested in their differential and categorical structure and in the geometry encoded by higher holonomies.

Topological field theory

3BF theory & TQFT

I study 3BF theory both in the continuum and on triangulated manifolds, including its gauge symmetries, Hamiltonian formulation, state-sum amplitudes, topological invariants and its formulation as a TQFT.

Quantum gravity

Gravity and matter from constrained BF theories

Higher BF theories provide a common setting in which gravitational and matter degrees of freedom can be introduced through constraints. My work includes scalar and gauge-field sectors and the relation between higher-gauge variables and physical fields.

Representation theory

Representations of 2-groups and 3-groups

A current direction is the representation theory of higher groups and its application to 3BF theory, including possible higher-group analogues of harmonic-analysis tools such as Peter–Weyl and Plancherel decompositions.

Discrete geometry

Triangulations, state sums & geometric variables

I am interested in discrete higher-gauge models on triangulations and in how algebraic variables used in state-sum formulations are related to geometric data such as simplices, areas, volumes and edge lengths.

Structure

Gauge symmetry & constrained systems

The gauge structure of higher BF theories is unusually rich. I study its canonical description, constraints and reducibility, and how these structures change when topological theories are constrained to describe local physical degrees of freedom.
