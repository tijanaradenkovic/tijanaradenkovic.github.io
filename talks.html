21 February 2025 · Institute of Physics Belgrade

3BF theory as a topological quantum field theory

Seminar talk presented at the Belgrade Group for Gravitation, Particles and Fields (GPF), Institute of Physics Belgrade.

Seminar page

Abstract

We study the path integral quantization of the topological 3BF theory, whose gauge symmetry is described by a 3-group. This theory is relevant for the quantization of general relativity coupled to Standard Model of elementary particles. We explicitly construct a state sum corresponding to the discretized path integral of a 3BF action. Being a topological invariant of 4-dimensional manifolds with boundary, this state sum gives rise to a topological quantum field theory (TQFT), realized as a functor between the category of cobordisms and the category of Hilbert spaces. After an introduction to appropriate category theory concepts and the construction of the state sum, we provide an explicit proof that it satisfies all Atiyah's axioms, and thus represents a genuine TQFT. The formulation of this TQFT represents a major step in the spinfoam quantization programme for a realistic theory of quantum gravity with matter.

29 January 2021 · Online

Gauge symmetry of the 3BF theory for a generic Lie 3-group

Seminar talk at the Belgrade Group for Gravitation, Particles and Fields (GPF), Institute of Physics Belgrade.

Seminar page

Abstract

The higher category theory can be employed to generalize the BF action to the so-called 3BF action, by passing from the notion of a gauge group to the notion of a gauge 3-group. In this work we determine the full gauge symmetry of the 3BF action. To that end, the complete Hamiltonian analysis of the 3BF action for a general Lie 3-group is performed, by using the Dirac procedure. This analysis is the first step towards a canonical quantization of a 3BF theory. This is an important stepping-stone for the quantization of the complete Standard Model of elementary particles coupled to Einstein–Cartan gravity, formulated as a 3BF action with suitable simplicity constraints. We show that the resulting gauge symmetry group consists of the already familiar G-, H-, and L-gauge transformations, as well as additional M- and N-gauge transformations, which have not been discussed in the existing literature.

13 October 2021 · Online

Topological higher gauge theory — from 2BF to 3BF theory

Seminar talk presented as part of the TQFT Seminar Series.

View slides (PDF) · Seminar page

Abstract

We study a generalization of BF-theories in the context of higher gauge theory. We construct a topological state sum Z, based on the classical 3BF action for a general semistrict Lie 3-group and a triangulation of a 4-dimensional spacetime manifold. The 3BF action is constructed using a 2-crossed module which encodes a 3-group (as introduced by Picken and Faria Martins), while the state sum Z is an example of Porter’s TQFT for d = 4 and n = 3. In order to verify that the constructed state sum is a topological invariant of the underlying manifold, its behavior under Pachner moves is analyzed, and it is obtained that the state sum Z remains the same. Our results are a generalization of the work done by Girelli, Pfeiffer, and Popescu for the case of state sum based on the classical 2BF action with the underlying 2-group structure.

References: J. Faria Martins and R. Picken, Diff. Geom. Appl. 29, 179 (2011), arXiv:0907.2566; T. Porter, J. Lond. Math. Soc. (2) 58, No. 3, 723 (1998), MR 1678163; F. Girelli, H. Pfeiffer and E. M. Popescu, J. Math. Phys. 49, 032503 (2008), arXiv:0708.3051.

19 July 2019 · Institute of Physics Belgrade, room 360

Construction of one-particle states in a curved spacetime in the case of Klein-Gordon field

Seminar talk at the Belgrade Group for Gravitation, Particles and Fields (GPF).

Abstract

We review how quantum field theory of a free Klein–Gordon field in Minkowski spacetime can be formulated without using a plane-wave expansion, a reformulation that is crucial for constructing QFT in curved spacetime. The discussion then turns to globally hyperbolic spacetimes, where the space of solutions to the classical field equations retains the basic structure familiar from Minkowski spacetime. Finally, following Wald, we consider how a physically sensible and fully deterministic evolution prescription can be given for a Klein–Gordon scalar field in an arbitrary nonglobally hyperbolic static spacetime.

References: R. M. Wald, Quantum Field Theory in Curved Spacetime and Black Hole Thermodynamics, University of Chicago Press (1994); R. M. Wald, “Dynamics in nonglobally hyperbolic static space-times,” J. Math. Phys. 21, 12 (1980).

25 May 2018 · Institute of Physics Belgrade, room 360

Action for scalar, Dirac and gauge fields as 3-BF action with constraints

Seminar talk at the Belgrade Group for Gravitation, Particles and Fields (GPF).

Abstract

First, we will remind you of the 2-BF model of quantum gravity — a 2-categorical generalization of the BF model — and the notion of a 2-group (equivalent to a crossed module) needed to construct it. We then consider the crossed module with G = SO(3,1) × U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3), instead of G = SO(3,1), obtaining equations of motion for gauge fields coupled to gravity as in Einstein–Cartan theory. Finally, using the notion of a 3-group, we show how the action for gravity coupled to scalar and Dirac fields can be written as a constrained 3-BF action.
