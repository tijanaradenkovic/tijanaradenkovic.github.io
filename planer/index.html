Account

You can use all planner features without registering. In guest mode, your data is stored only in this browser on this device; clearing browser storage may delete it. To save your data to your account in the cloud and synchronize across devices, create an account or sign in. You can export a JSON backup at any time.

Install as an app: in Chrome or Edge click the install icon in the address bar (or menu → Install). On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Research activity export

Create a formatted summary of your papers, presentations, conferences, visits, seminars and grants for a CV, annual report or website. Choose a period and format, then copy or save the result. This is different from Export JSON above: JSON is a full planner backup for restoring or moving your editable data; these formats are for sharing and reporting, not restoring your planner.

Papers are dated by when you moved them to their current stage (accepted, published...), presentations by their date or the conference start.
