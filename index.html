About

Geometry, symmetry and quantum spacetime

I am a theoretical physicist at the Institute of Physics Belgrade. My research focuses on mathematical structures for quantum gravity, particularly higher gauge theories based on 2-groups and 3-groups, BF-type theories, and topological invariants of manifolds.

I completed my PhD in Physics at the University of Belgrade in 2023 with a thesis on higher gauge theories and quantum gravity.
